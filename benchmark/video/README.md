# Video benchmark assets

This directory is reserved for a future video benchmark. Add only assets you own or are licensed to evaluate and redistribute.

- `real/`: independently verified unmanipulated source videos.
- `ai/`: independently verified AI-generated/deepfake videos.
- `manipulated/`: edited/manipulated videos with documented manipulation ground truth.
- `compressed/`: transcoded or forwarded stress cases. Record their original ground truth and transformations separately.

The current image benchmark runner does not evaluate these directories, and no video benchmark runner, measured accuracy, or calibration artifact is included. Do not publish a video accuracy claim until a video-specific evaluation protocol and labeled data have been validated.
